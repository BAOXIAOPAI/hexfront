## Researched audio reference

[Casino Audio by Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio) was reviewed as an optional CC0 source. Its download was unavailable during production, and **no Kenney audio is included or used** in this project's current assets. The original synthesized sounds above are the actual shipped sounds.

## Game presentation

The resource icons and synthesized sounds listed here are independently produced reusable assets. No CATAN printed card scans or CATAN logos are included in this asset selection.

The launch cover (`voyage-cover.webp`) is an original AI-generated archipelago illustration created for this project.
